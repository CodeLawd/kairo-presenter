# Bundled Bible sources

## Berean Standard Bible (BSB)

The Berean Standard Bible text is dedicated to the public domain. The bundled
verse database was generated from the publisher's official `bsb.txt` download:

- https://berean.bible/downloads.htm
- https://berean.bible/terms.htm

Downloaded for this build on 2026-08-23. The official text was imported without
modification.

## King James Version (KJV) and Bible in Basic English (BBE)

These public-domain texts were imported by `scripts/seed-bible.ts` from the
Thiago Bodruk Bible dataset.
